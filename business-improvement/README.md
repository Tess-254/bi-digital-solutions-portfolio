# Business Improvement Projects

## Overview

This section demonstrates how data analysis, business intelligence and digital solutions can address operational challenges, improve reporting processes and support better organizational decisions.

## Projects

### 1. Reporting Process Optimization
**Status:** Planned

A process improvement project focused on identifying repetitive reporting tasks, reducing manual steps and improving the consistency and timeliness of reporting.

**Planned tools:** Power BI, Excel, SharePoint and Power Automate.

### 2. Fundraising Data Intelligence Hub
**Status:** Planned

A solution concept for organizing organizational statistics, programme results and supporting evidence in a centralized resource to support proposal development and fundraising.

**Planned tools:** SharePoint, Power BI and Power Automate.

### 3. KPI Monitoring and Decision Support
**Status:** Planned

An analytical solution concept for monitoring key performance indicators, identifying performance gaps and highlighting areas that require management attention.

**Planned tools:** Power BI, SQL, Power Query and DAX.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Business problem analysis
- Requirements gathering
- Process mapping and improvement
- KPI development
- Reporting optimization
- Stakeholder engagement
- Solution design and testing
- Benefits identification and measurement

## Project Documentation

Each case study will describe the business problem, current process, root causes, proposed solution, implementation approach and expected or measured outcomes.

Any impact claims will be supported by actual evidence. Where results have not yet been measured, expected benefits will be clearly identified.

*Note: Projects marked as planned are not yet completed or deployed.*
